## Almanac Researcher Agent — Refined Plan

### Goal
A research agent that populates or improves almanac entries for any food item
(vegetables, fruit, fish, mushrooms) with Chicago/Illinois seasonal data.

---

### Inputs
- A food item name or existing almanac ID.
- Agent reads `src/server/data/almanac.json` first to check whether the item exists.

---

### Two modes

**Create** — item not in almanac:
Produce a full new entry object.

**Update** — item already in almanac:
Read the existing entry. Return only the fields that need changing; set unchanged
fields to `null`. Do not rewrite for style.

---

### Output schema

```json
{
  "id": "<kebab-case-id>",
  "name": "<Display Name>",
  "months": ["<MonthName>"],
  "peakMonths": ["<MonthName>"],
  "description": "<string>"
}
```

Full month names only (`January`–`December`). No abbreviations.

`peakMonths` = window of best quality, not just earliest availability.

---

### description field
4 sentences, one per slot:
1. Flavor, texture, and aroma at peak season.
2. Why locally sourced tastes different — adapt by category:
   - Vegetables / fruit: climate, soil, or growing conditions (e.g. frost sweetening, lake-effect humidity, heavy clay soil).
   - Fish: lake or river conditions, water temperature, seasonal migration or spawning cycles.
   - Mushrooms: foraging habitat, moisture and temperature patterns, forest type.
3. How flavor shifts across the season — what the item is like before peak, at peak, and after (e.g. starchier early, sweetest at frost, bitter late).
4. What a quality specimen looks like and how to choose one.

Factual and direct. No metaphor or marketing language. 100–120 words. If a slot cannot be filled with verified data, omit that sentence rather than speculating.

---

### Search budget
- Run WebSearch before WebFetch. If the snippet answers the question, stop.
- Max 6 WebSearch calls and 2 WebFetch calls per item.
- Preferred sources: Illinois Extension, USDA, Great Lakes fishery reports,
  regional foraging guides.
- Do not invent data. If uncertain, omit the claim.

---

### What to avoid
- Sourcing / where to buy (farmers markets, stores, CSAs) — out of scope.
- `notes` and `origin` fields — not used in this schema.
- Rewriting values that are already accurate.
